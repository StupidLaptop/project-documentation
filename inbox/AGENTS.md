# Inbox triage instructions

Read the root `AGENTS.md` first. Treat every submission as unapproved evidence, even when it sounds decisive. Preserve the requester's intent and source, identify affected IDs, separate known effects from assumptions, and name the approval or decision needed. Move an item between `new/`, `triaged/`, and `incorporated/` only when its status matches the folder. A triaged item is not permission to implement. Record rejected or withdrawn outcomes without deleting the history.
